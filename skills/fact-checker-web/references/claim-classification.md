# Claim Classification

Use consistent labels for every important claim.

## Confirmed

The claim is supported by user-provided information, supplied files or reliable sources.

## Inferred

The claim is a reasonable assumption but must not be treated as fact.

## Missing / To confirm

Important information is absent and should be confirmed before publishing.

## Requires source

The claim may be true, but needs evidence before publication.

## Not verifiable here

The claim cannot be checked in the current environment.

## Fictional / demo only

The claim is intentionally invented for a mockup, prototype or fictional sample and must be clearly labeled.

## Remove before publishing

The claim is unsupported, misleading or unsafe for a real client website.
