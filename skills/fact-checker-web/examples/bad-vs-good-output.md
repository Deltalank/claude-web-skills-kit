# Bad vs Good Output

## Bad output

```text
Everything sounds fine.
```

Why this is bad:

- no scope
- no claim table
- no missing data section
- no source requirements
- unsupported certainty

## Good output

```text
Confirmed:
- Business type: local gym

Missing / To confirm:
- opening hours
- address
- current prices
- verified social proof

Requires source:
- strongest local claims
- exact rating claims

Safe rewrite:
- "Local gym focused on strength training and personal support."
```

Why this is good:

- separates facts from missing data
- marks claims needing evidence
- gives safer wording
- avoids unsupported certainty
