# Privacy and Cookies

Review privacy whenever a site collects, stores or sends personal data.

## Check for

- contact forms
- newsletter forms
- WhatsApp links with prefilled messages
- analytics scripts
- maps
- videos
- tracking pixels
- cookies
- localStorage
- sessionStorage
- personal data in HTML or JavaScript

## Common risk areas

- forms without privacy notice
- analytics without consent handling where required
- embedded third-party resources without disclosure
- generic privacy policy with unconfirmed legal data
- collecting phone numbers or health-related information without clear purpose

## Rule

This skill does not provide legal advice. It flags privacy and cookie risks and marks legal compliance as requiring human/legal review.
