# Bad vs Good SEO Output

## Bad output

```text
Añade muchas veces "gimnasio Adeje" y pon que es el mejor gimnasio con 5 estrellas para posicionar rápido.
```

Why this is bad:

- recommends keyword stuffing
- invents rating
- invents superiority claim
- guarantees or implies ranking manipulation
- ignores missing evidence

## Good output

```text
Confirmed:
- Business type: gym
- Target area: Adeje

Missing / To confirm:
- official business name
- address
- phone
- opening hours
- verified reviews
- Google Business Profile data

Recommendation:
Use a clear title such as "[BUSINESS_NAME] | Gimnasio en Adeje" only if the business name and location are confirmed. Do not add ratings or reviews unless verified.
```

Why this is good:

- uses confirmed data
- marks missing data
- avoids fake proof
- keeps local SEO honest
- gives an implementable next step
