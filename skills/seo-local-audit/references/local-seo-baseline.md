# Local SEO Baseline

Use this baseline for local business websites.

## Core checks

- Business type is clear.
- Target location or service area is clear.
- Primary service is clear.
- Main CTA is clear.
- Title tag is specific.
- Meta description is specific and useful.
- H1 communicates the main offer.
- Headings follow a logical structure.
- NAP data is confirmed or marked as missing.
- Google Business Profile alignment is considered.
- Reviews and ratings are verified before use.
- Schema uses only confirmed facts.
- Sitemap and robots are present or planned.
- Page is mobile-friendly enough for local users.

## Avoid

- Keyword stuffing.
- Fake reviews.
- Fake ratings.
- Fake awards.
- Guaranteed ranking claims.
- Doorway pages.
- Pages with only location-name swaps and no real value.
