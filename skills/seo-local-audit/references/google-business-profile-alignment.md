# Google Business Profile Alignment

A local business website should support the same real-world information that appears in the business profile.

## Check alignment for

- business name
- business category or service type
- address or service area
- phone number
- website URL
- opening hours
- services
- photos
- reviews and proof
- contact path

## Rules

- Do not invent profile data.
- Do not claim profile ownership unless confirmed.
- Do not use fake reviews or fake ratings.
- Mark profile details as pending verification if the user has not provided them.

## Why this matters

Local SEO depends heavily on trust, consistency and clarity. The website should not contradict the business profile.
