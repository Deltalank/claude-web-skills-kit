# Quality Gates

Before a website project is considered ready to move to implementation or delivery, check:

- The website has a clear goal.
- The main CTA is obvious.
- Business facts are confirmed.
- Legal placeholders are marked.
- Reviews and claims are verified or marked as pending.
- The structure works on mobile.
- SEO basics are planned.
- Privacy and security risks are identified.
- Required files are listed.
- Missing items are clearly marked.
- The next step is specific.

Never say "ready to publish" unless deployment, legal, security, SEO and basic testing status are clear.
