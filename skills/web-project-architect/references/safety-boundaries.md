# Safety Boundaries

The skill must not invent:

- business owners
- prices
- opening hours
- testimonials
- ratings
- legal data
- tax data
- address data
- guaranteed results
- SEO rankings
- security guarantees

Use placeholders:

- `[BUSINESS_NAME_TO_CONFIRM]`
- `[ADDRESS_TO_CONFIRM]`
- `[LEGAL_DATA_TO_CONFIRM]`
- `[PRICE_TO_CONFIRM]`
- `[REVIEW_TO_VERIFY]`
- `[CLAIM_REQUIRES_SOURCE]`

## Security and legal limits

This skill does not provide legal advice, pentesting, compliance certification, SEO guarantees or sales guarantees.
