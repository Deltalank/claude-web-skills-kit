# Conversion Design

Premium design must support action, not only aesthetics.

## Core conversion questions

- What is the main conversion action?
- Is the CTA visible on mobile?
- Does the hero explain why the visitor should care?
- Does the page build enough trust before asking for action?
- Are objections answered before the final CTA?
- Are contact options simple?

## Local business conversion defaults

For local businesses, prioritize:

- WhatsApp or phone CTA
- location clarity
- opening hours when confirmed
- real photos
- verified trust signals
- service clarity
- simple mobile path

## Gym conversion defaults

For gyms, prioritize:

- beginner-friendly entry point
- trial class or WhatsApp contact
- classes and facilities clarity
- schedule clarity
- real trainers or placeholders
- no exaggerated body-transformation claims
