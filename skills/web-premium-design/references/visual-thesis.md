# Visual Thesis

Every premium web design proposal must define a visual thesis.

## Required questions

- What should the visitor feel in the first 3 seconds?
- What should the visitor understand immediately?
- What should the visitor do next?
- Why does this design fit the business?
- What makes it different from a generic template?

## Example

For a local gym landing page:

- feeling: energy, trust and local proximity
- understanding: this gym is nearby and easy to contact
- next action: WhatsApp or visit
- fit: the design should feel active but not fake or exaggerated
- differentiation: real local proof and clear schedule beat generic fitness slogans
