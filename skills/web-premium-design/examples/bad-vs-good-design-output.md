# Bad vs Good Design Output

## Bad output

```text
Use a modern gradient, add cards, add testimonials and make it premium.
```

Why this is bad:

- no visual thesis
- generic advice
- invents testimonials
- no mobile or CTA strategy
- no trust strategy
- no link to business goal

## Good output

```text
Visual thesis:
The page should feel energetic, local and trustworthy within the first 3 seconds. The visitor should understand that this is a nearby gym and the next action is WhatsApp contact.

Confirmed:
- Business type: gym
- Goal: WhatsApp contact

Missing:
- real photos
- opening hours
- verified reviews

Design direction:
Use a strong dark athletic base, high-contrast CTA, real facility imagery and a compact mobile-first hero.
```

Why this is good:

- defines a thesis
- ties design to conversion
- marks missing data
- avoids fake proof
- gives implementable direction
